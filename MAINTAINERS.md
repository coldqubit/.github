# Maintainers

This file lists who maintains the **coldqubit project home** (the org, its shared
standards, and this repository) and points at each project's own roster. How decisions
are made lives in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Home maintainers

| Maintainer | Role | Areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [coldqubit](https://github.com/coldqubit) | Lead maintainer | Org, shared standards, coldqubit.org |

There is one home maintainer today. That is stated plainly on purpose: the home is
built to take on more, not built around a single person. Written governance, recorded
decisions, and container-first reproducibility across the projects exist so that
nothing depends on any one person remaining available.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainership is per project first: land well-scoped pull requests, help triage and
review, and an existing maintainer proposes you in that project's roster (the path is
documented in each project's `MAINTAINERS.md`). Home maintainers are drawn from active
project maintainers by the same mechanism: a pull request against this file, confirmed
by lazy consensus.

Co-maintainers are welcome at every level, including for a single area such as a
backend, the Terraform module, or the docs.

## Contact

Reach the maintainers through the relevant project's issues, through
[this repository's issues](https://github.com/coldqubit/.github/issues) for org-level
matters, or at `info@coldqubit.org` for private ones. Security reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Emeritus

None yet. A maintainer who steps back is moved here with thanks, keeping credit for
past work and dropping only the review obligation.
